## Adaptation

The bundled Blender template was converted with Blender 2.93 for the GPT Director workflow.
The adaptation removes the original demonstration camera and set, retains the articulated
mannequin mesh, armature, IK/FK controls, drivers and custom controller shapes, and replaces
the original wood textures with recolorable director-stage materials. A legacy cyclic
spine-to-child-chest rotation constraint was removed for Blender 2.93 dependency-graph
compatibility; the chest, neck, head, shoulder and limb controls remain available.

When distributing renders or projects created with this asset, retain this attribution record.
