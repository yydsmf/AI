# Modern Furniture Pack for GPT Director

This bundled Blender 2.93-compatible furniture pack contains two independently
licensed CC0 assets downloaded from BlendSwap.

## Sofa Array Modern

- Asset: `Sofa Array Modern`
- Creator: LauroVictorDesign
- Source: https://blendswap.com/blend/26538
- License: CC0
- Source Blender version: 2.90

The Director pack keeps the two-seat sofa and its three cushions. The one-seat
and three-seat variants, source presentation objects, camera, and lights were
removed. Object origins and placement were normalized for the Director stage.

## Modern Table set

- Asset: `Modern Table set`
- Creator: Neobox
- Source: https://blendswap.com/blend/27096
- License: CC0
- Source Blender version: 2.91

The Director pack keeps the low round geometric coffee table from the `big table`
collection. The high table, cube table, ground, source camera, and source lights
were removed. The table was uniformly scaled to approximately 1.16 meters wide
and 0.50 meters high for dialogue scenes.

## Packaging notes

The assets were consolidated and saved with Blender 2.93.0. Their materials and
packed image dependencies are retained. Director-specific template roots and
source metadata were added so each prop can be placed, rotated, scaled, and
tracked as a controllable production asset.
